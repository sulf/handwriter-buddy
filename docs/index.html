How it works

1 · Write

Type your message. Single-stroke Hershey cursive, sized in real millimeters.

2 · Aim

Click the true-scale bed preview to place it. Add SVG drawings, move, scale, rotate.

3 · Plot

A pen on the printhead writes it — ink on paper, letter by letter.

Print the pen attachment

Six printable parts. Pick the adapter that fits your pen.

Cap closes the top of the tube 53 × 74 × 12 mm
Printed spring keeps gentle pressure on the pen 48 × 42 × 20 mm
Pen tube mounts on the toolhead 25 × 20 × 42 mm
Adapter S small pen bore — pick one 25 × 20 × 13 mm
Adapter M medium pen bore — pick one 25 × 20 × 24 mm
Adapter L large pen bore — pick one 25 × 20 × 53 mm
